# Zeren Café + Catering Website Refresh

## Outcome
Rebrand the current website as Zeren, a welcoming Oval café with a clear brunch-first identity, while retaining its catering quote and tasting request experience. Keep the current admin and ordering records accessible.

## Implementation
1. **Replace the old brand consistently**
   - Update the header, homepage, menu, gallery, footer, contact pages, browser/social titles, and visible image descriptions to use Zeren.
   - Build a distinctive wordmark and matching small icon around the supplied sage-green frontage and black sign description; use a restrained sage, deep ink, and fresh neutral palette.
   - Remove the old Mayfair address, phone, email, claims, and copy wherever they appear. Use only the supplied address: 82 Bolton Crescent, Oval, SE5 0SE. Leave phone, opening hours, and email unpublished until provided.

2. **Reshape the public pages for café discovery and catering**
   - Make the homepage lead with Zeren, its brunch offer, and clear paths to the menu, visit details, and catering enquiries.
   - Reorganize the menu around the verified offer in the brief: Afro-brunch, pancakes, French toast, Full English, matchas, mocktails, and coffee. Keep existing basket controls; do not present checkout as online payment unless that capability is separately enabled.
   - Use the gallery and real dining photography to show food, the storefront, and atmosphere; keep the catering quote and private-tasting journey available from the main navigation.
   - Preserve the admin dashboard, customer records, existing order history, and inquiry handling.

3. **Use only verified media and business claims**
   - Review available image files and use them in their appropriate editorial, gallery, or menu contexts. Do not use guest-containing photography as menu product photography.
   - The uploaded analysis refers to separate storefront and dish photos plus two videos, but those source files are not among the currently attached files. Use no fabricated substitutes; incorporate them after they are supplied. Confirm usage permission and remove social-screen interface elements without removing creator attribution.
   - Display a Food Hygiene rating only after verifying it is current and using an official badge asset. Do not claim halal certification or unconfirmed allergens.

4. **Retain catering without carrying over unverified terms**
   - Keep the guest-count, event-style, service-level, booking, and tasting-request workflows.
   - Treat current quote rates, minimum spend, tasting fee/complimentary threshold, and tasting terms as unconfirmed for Zeren; keep them unpublished or unchanged until confirmed for this business.
   - Keep event inquiries and tasting selections visible to administrators, and preserve the existing confirmation behavior without promising email delivery that is not configured.

5. **Validate the finished experience**
   - Check all public pages and navigation on desktop and mobile, menu filtering and basket actions, quote calculations and submissions, gallery viewing, dashboard access, and brand metadata.
   - Confirm no old Canapés & Treats branding, Mayfair address, or unverified contact/food claims remain in public pages.
