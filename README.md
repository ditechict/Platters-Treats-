**A modern, sleek, international-style restaurant website for a UK-based brand named *“Canapés and Treats Pretty Platters.”***  
The restaurant specializes in elegant canapés, stylish platters, and indulgent treats that appeal to both local and international audiences. The overall design should be sophisticated yet approachable, reflecting an upscale but welcoming dining and catering experience.

**Key Features & Requirements:**

1. **Brand Identity & Look:**

   * Modern and minimal design with elegant typography.
   * Color palette: muted neutrals (black, white, ivory) with accents of gold or soft pastels.
   * Full-width hero image/video showcasing artistic food platters.
   * Clean layout, ample white space, sleek icons, and subtle animations for a premium feel.

2. **Homepage:**

   * Restaurant name and tagline in a refined font.
   * Hero banner with background video/image of styled food platters.
   * Sections for:

     * “About Us” (short story about the brand).
     * “Our Specialties” (signature platters & treats).
     * “Order Now” call-to-action button leading to the ecommerce cart.

3. **Menu Page:**

   * Dedicated **Menu Items** page showcasing canapés, treats, and platters.
   * Each item should have:

     * High-quality image.
     * Description (ingredients, style).
     * Price.
     * “Add to Cart” button.

4. **Ecommerce Functionality:**

   * Simple and intuitive shopping cart system.
   * Users can select items from the menu and place orders online.
   * Cart should include item count, total price, and checkout button.
   * Integration of secure UK-based payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).

5. **Additional Pages:**

   * **Contact Page**: phone, email, map, and a simple contact form.
   * **Gallery**: curated photos of events, platters, and treats.
   * **Testimonials/Reviews** section for credibility.

6. **User Experience:**

   * Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
   * International appeal with clean navigation and multilingual readiness (English first, optional expansion).
   * Fast loading times, optimized images.

7. **Tone & Vibe:**

   * Sleek, global, upscale dining and catering brand.
   * Focused on presentation, taste, and indulgence.
   * Feels “Instagrammable” but also professional.
